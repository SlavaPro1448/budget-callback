Budget iOS — Privacy Notice

Budget iOS is a personal finance application used by its author for his own accounts only. It is not distributed to the public.

Account information obtained through Enable Banking (PSD2 account information service) is stored only on the user's own device (and optionally in the user's own iCloud private database). No data is transferred to any third party and no server operated by the author receives it.

Access to bank accounts can be revoked at any time in the application («Подключённые счета → Отключить») or at the bank.
